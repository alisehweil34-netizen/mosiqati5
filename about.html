🎵 About Us

Our story, vision, and passion for music since 2014

Our Story

MOSIQATI's journey began in 2014 when founder Ahmad Al-Zayadi decided to turn his passion for music into a real business. The store launched from a small room in downtown Amman with just three types of instruments and a single shelf.

Within two years, the store expanded to a full shop on Rainbow Street in Shmeisani, joined by a team of young musicians who share the same mission: making music accessible to every Jordanian.

Today we are proud to be Jordan's #1 music instrument reference, with over 500 products and 2,000 happy customers across all governorates.

Our Journey Through the Years

From a small room to Jordan's #1 store

2014
🚀 MOSIQATI Founded

Opening of the first store in downtown Amman, 20 square meters with a limited selection of guitars and keyboards. A humble start to a big dream.

2016
📈 First Expansion

Moved to a larger location in Shmeisani and added oud and drums sections. Reached 300 customers and hired the first 3 specialist staff members.

2018
🌐 Website Launch

Launched the MOSIQATI website to reach customers from all Jordanian governorates. Online sales exceeded our expectations by 200%.

2020
🎓 MOSIQATI Academy Launch

Opened a teaching section in the store offering guitar, piano and oud lessons for beginners. Over 150 students graduated in the first year.

2022
🏆 Best Music Store Award

MOSIQATI received the "Best Specialized Store" award from the Amman Chamber of Commerce, officially recognizing our position in the Jordanian market.

2024
✨ Ten Years of Music

Celebrating ten years of passion and success with over 2,000 loyal customers and a catalog of 500+ music products from 50+ global brands.

Our Vision, Mission & Values

🔭
Our Vision

To be the most trusted first reference for music instruments in Jordan and the Arab region, contributing to building a generation that expresses itself through music.

📜
Our Mission

Providing the best music instruments at fair and competitive prices, with specialized technical support that guarantees every customer's satisfaction from their first visit through after-purchase.

💎
Quality First

We do not sell any product without inspecting it and verifying it meets international standards. Every instrument we display has first been approved by our music experts.

🤝
Honesty & Transparency

We believe in honesty with our customers. We guide you to what suits your level and budget, even if that means suggesting a lower-priced product.

🌱
Supporting the Music Community

We support young Jordanian musicians and provide them a platform to express their talents through our regular music events.

♻️
Social Responsibility

We annually donate music instruments to public schools and offer special discounts to music institute students and people with special needs.
